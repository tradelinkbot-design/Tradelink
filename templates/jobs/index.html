The Verified badge is awarded after we confirm your identity and at least one skill credential (e.g. trade certificate, references from a previous employer, or a short skills test). Verification boosts your profile visibility significantly and builds trust with employers. Apply via your profile settings.

Register an employer account, complete your company profile, then click "Post a Job" from your dashboard. Fill in the trade category, location, pay, and requirements — your listing goes live within minutes and is immediately visible to thousands of verified tradespeople. Get started free.

Workers with the Verified badge have passed our identity and credential check. Beyond that, you can review each worker's star ratings, reviews from past employers, portfolio photos, and years of experience — all displayed on their public profile before you make contact.

Posting jobs and browsing worker profiles is free. Premium employer plans unlock features like promoted job listings, unlimited direct messaging, and advanced analytics. See our pricing page for full details.

We currently cover all 36 Nigerian states plus the FCT. Our highest concentrations of jobs and workers are currently in Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Enugu, Kano, and Ibadan — but the platform is growing rapidly in every region.

We take data privacy seriously. Your personal information is encrypted, never sold to third parties, and only shared with employers when you apply to their jobs. We comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR). See our Privacy Policy for full details.
