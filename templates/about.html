The Nigerian Reality

The Informal Economy's Hidden Crisis

Over 80% of Nigeria's workforce operates without formal digital identity. Skilled artisans remain invisible beyond their street corner while employers face daily risks: unverified workers, abandoned projects, and zero payment protection.

  • No verification: Anyone can claim a trade title — exposing homes to faulty wiring, leaking pipes, and structural risks.
  • Payment anxiety: Artisans chase unpaid invoices while clients lose deposits to vanishing contractors.
  • Zero digital reach: Master craftsmen with 20 years of skill remain unknown beyond their neighbourhood.
The TradeLink Answer

A Trusted Ecosystem Built for Nigeria

TradeLink NG removes the trust deficit from every transaction. We give artisans a verified digital identity and employers a structured hiring system — both protected by milestone escrow and human dispute resolution.

  • Milestone escrow: Funds are deposited before work begins and released only when milestones are inspected and approved.

FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about using TradeLink NG safely and effectively.

Why is Milestone Escrow safer than Pay on Delivery?
Pay on Delivery leaves the artisan uncertain about payment and the client exposed to uncompleted work. Escrow locks the client's money in a neutral vault before work starts — the artisan works knowing payment is secured, and the client's funds don't move until they approve the completed milestone.
How are artisans verified on TradeLink NG?
Our team manually reviews National Identification Numbers (NIN), verified phone numbers, trade apprenticeship records, professional certifications and, for businesses, CAC registration documents. Each level of evidence earns a corresponding Blue, Gold or Green trust badge.
Can construction companies hire entire trade crews?
Yes. Real estate developers, facility managers and construction firms use TradeLink NG to hire multi-trade crews — welders, tilers, painters, electricians — all under milestone contracts that keep large projects on budget and on schedule.
What happens when there is a dispute on a job?
Either party can trigger our Dispute Resolution process from the milestone contract page. Because funds are held in escrow, our Nigerian support team reviews agreed terms, photos and communications to mediate fairly — without either honest party losing money.
Is TradeLink NG free to join as a technician?
Yes. Creating a technician profile, browsing jobs and submitting bids is completely free. A small platform service fee is only deducted from successfully completed and approved milestone payouts — no win, no fee.
