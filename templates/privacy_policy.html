1 Who We Are

TradeLink NG ("we", "us", "our", or "the Company") is a digital platform connecting verified skilled tradespeople — including electricians, plumbers, carpenters, welders, technicians, and construction artisans — with clients, homeowners, and commercial employers across Nigeria.

Our services encompass: a job board for posting and managing work opportunities; a verified artisan directory; a marketplace for trade tools and equipment; a direct messaging service; a secure escrow payment mechanism; and a structured identity verification process.

We operate as the data controller regarding the personal information collected through our web applications and digital communication channels.

2 Data We Collect

2.1 Account Registration Data

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Full name (first name, last name)
  • Email address — used as your primary account identifier
  • Username — public display name
  • Phone number — required for contact verification and service notices
  • Profile photo — optional, uploaded to assist identity confirmation
  • Password credentials — securely transformed using robust, one-way cryptographic algorithms; we never store or have access to plain-text passwords

If you choose to sign in via authorized third-party authentication providers, we receive profile information (such as your name and verified email address) solely as permitted by your settings with that provider.

2.2 Worker Profile Data

Artisans and technicians may choose to provide professional details:

  • Trade classifications and specialized technical skills
  • Years of professional experience and trade credentials
  • Professional summary and service descriptions
  • Operational State and Local Government Area (LGA) in Nigeria
  • Billing rates and pricing structures in Naira (₦)
  • Work availability, scheduling preferences, and relocation preferences
  • Portfolio media displaying verified project history
  • Professional references and verified certificates

2.3 Employer & Client Profile Data

  • Business, company, or individual client name
  • Industry classification and operational location
  • Job descriptions, project specifications, timelines, and budgets

2.4 Marketplace & Transaction Information

  • Tool and equipment listings (specifications, photographs, pricing, condition)
  • Order, transaction, and contract history
  • Agreed service terms, milestones, and deliverables
  • Dispute documentation and records required for formal mediation

2.5 Communication Records

  • In-platform messages and file attachments exchanged regarding projects or transactions
  • Message delivery status and interaction timestamps
  • Customer support enquiries and communications across our official channels

2.6 Identity Verification Data (Sensitive)

To promote safety and eliminate impersonation, users may voluntarily complete identity verification:

  • National Identification Number (NIN) — securely transmitted to accredited government-authorized identity verification providers for confirmation; we retain solely the last 4 digits for administrative audit
  • Bank Verification Number (BVN) — verified through accredited banking identity gateways for financial account validation; only the last 4 digits are retained
  • Corporate Registration (CAC) — official registration number and entity name for business accounts
  • Transaction reference identifiers and validation timestamps confirming identity status

🔒 Data Minimization Standard: Complete identification numbers (full NIN or full BVN) are never permanently stored in our active application databases. Transmissions occur strictly through encrypted channels directly to accredited verification partners.

2.7 Technical & Operational Logs

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses utilized for access controls and fraud mitigation
  • Device parameters, operating system, and browser classifications
  • Platform interactions, page navigation, and search queries
  • Aggregated performance, security, and diagnostic data

5 Identity Verification (KYC)

TradeLink NG offers a multi-tier trust and safety verification model:

  • Level 1 — Identity Verified: Validated against official national identity databases.
  • Level 2 — Enhanced Financial Verification: Validated against official banking identity registries for escrow transactions.
  • Level 3 — Verified Business: Validated against corporate registry databases for registered commercial entities.

All verification processing is conducted via accredited, licensed identity verification partners operating in Nigeria. We receive confirmation tokens indicating verification status without archiving sensitive full credential numbers on our application servers.

6 Payments & Escrow

Financial transactions are administered through licensed, Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN)-regulated payment service providers.

  • TradeLink NG does not store full credit card numbers, sensitive card verification values (CVV), or banking PINs.
  • Escrow deposits remain under the custody of licensed payment partners until the designated project milestones are authorized by the client.
  • Our platform maintains transaction references, timestamps, and settlement amounts for legal auditing, taxation compliance, and dispute mediation.
  • Financial transaction histories are preserved in accordance with statutory accounting and financial record retention regulations.

8 Automated Matching & Recommendations

To assist employers in locating qualified technicians quickly and ensure relevant opportunities reach artisans, TradeLink NG employs automated matching systems.

  • Job specifications (trade type, required skills, location) are algorithmically compared with worker profile descriptions and demonstrated capabilities.
  • Matching calculations occur within our secure platform infrastructure.
  • Profile information is not submitted to external public artificial intelligence platforms for training or data harvesting.
  • You retain full autonomy to apply for any job or decline any job matches regardless of algorithmic rankings.

9 Communications & Messaging

  • Communications conducted through the platform are transmitted over encrypted connections.
  • Messages are accessible strictly to the conversation participants, except where authorized platform moderators must review records in connection with a formal dispute or safety investigation.
  • Unsent media files uploaded to temporary staging are routinely purged.
  • Communications initiated through authorized messaging integrations are processed strictly to facilitate client-worker coordination.

11 Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and applicable data privacy regulations, you possess comprehensive data rights:

  • Right to Access: Request verification and copies of personal data held about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete personal details.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of your account and personal records, subject to mandatory legal and financial compliance retention periods.
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain a portable copy of information you provided directly.
  • Right to Object & Restrict: Request limitations on specific non-essential data processing.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent previously granted for optional features without affecting prior lawful processing.

Requests can be submitted directly to privacy@tradelink.ng. We process verified privacy requests within 30 days.

12 Children's Privacy

TradeLink NG is strictly designated for individuals who are 18 years of age or older. We do not intentionally collect or process data from minors. If you become aware that an individual under 18 has established an account, notify us immediately for investigation and account closure.

13 Information Security

We maintain comprehensive administrative, organizational, and technical safeguards designed to preserve data confidentiality, integrity, and availability:

  • Transport Layer Security: All data transmitted between client devices and our platform is encrypted using modern cryptographic protocols (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Storage Protections: Platform databases and persistent storage volumes utilize robust encryption-at-rest standards.
  • Authentication Controls: User credentials are protected using industry-standard one-way password hashing techniques.
  • Access Governance: Administrative capabilities are strictly segregated based on minimum necessary privilege principles, requiring multi-factor authentication.
  • Defensive Controls: Automated traffic filtering, input validation, CSRF defense, and rate-limiting protections are active to mitigate unauthorized access attempts.
  • Incident Management: In the unlikely event of a security incident affecting personal data, notifications will be delivered to affected users and regulatory bodies within mandatory statutory timeframes.
