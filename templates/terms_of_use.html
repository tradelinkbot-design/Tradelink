9 Escrow & Payments

9.1 How Escrow Works

TradeLink NG operates through licensed, Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN)-regulated escrow payment partners. The process for marketplace transactions:

  1. Buyer initiates payment → funds held securely in escrow
  2. Both parties meet and hand over the item
  3. Buyer confirms receipt on the platform
  4. TradeLink NG deducts a 5% platform fee; remaining amount transferred to seller
  5. Transaction automatically completes 7 days after buyer confirmation (if no dispute is raised)

10 Disputes & Mediation

10.1 Raising a Dispute

Either party (buyer or seller) may raise a dispute on a completed or in-progress order. Disputes must be raised within 7 days of the buyer's confirmation of receipt (or within 7 days of the agreed meetup date if receipt was never confirmed).

10.2 Mediation Period

Upon a dispute being raised, both parties have 5 days to reach a mutually agreed resolution within the platform's mediation tool. During this period, escrow funds remain frozen.

10.3 Admin Resolution

If mediation fails, TradeLink NG's admin team will review the dispute within 72 hours of escalation. Our team will consider:

  • Chat message history between the parties
  • Uploaded evidence (photos, receipts, documents)
  • Transaction timestamps and order history
  • Both parties' accounts of events

Possible resolutions include: full release to seller, full refund to buyer, or a split settlement. Our decision is final and binding.

10.4 Auto-Release

If no dispute is raised within 7 days of buyer confirmation, escrow funds are automatically released to the seller. If a buyer fails to confirm or deny receipt within 14 days of the agreed meetup, funds are automatically released to the seller.
