1. Executive Technical Summary & Problem Statement

Over 80% of Nigeria's employment is generated within the informal economy (NBS/ILO data). However, millions of skilled artisans (electricians, plumbers, solar installers, welders, masons) suffer from severe economic invisibility, lack of verifiable credentials, and persistent payment default risks. Conversely, property owners, corporate site supervisors, and facility managers face unvetted technical quacks, property damage, and abandoned projects.

TradeLink NG solves this systemic trust deficit by providing a battle-tested, end-to-end digital framework combining: (1) NIN-backed multi-tier credential verification, (2) milestone-locked financial escrow, (3) AI-powered semantic and visual portfolio matching, and (4) an omnichannel WhatsApp Business gateway designed for low-bandwidth informal workers.

3. Core Technical Modules & Workflows

Pillar A: Multi-Tier Verification System (Trust Badges)

To dismantle identity fraud and incompetent workmanship, TradeLink NG operates a 3-tier progressive verification system, granting distinct cryptographic and visual badges on artisan profiles:

Badge Tier Requirements & Verification Source Rights & Project Scope
BLUE BADGE
Level 1: Identity		 • National Identification Number (NIN)
• Phone number OTP confirmation
• Live selfie face match		 Eligible for residential repairs, neighborhood gigs, and marketplace listings.
GOLD BADGE
Level 2: Certified Trade		 • Blue Badge requirements +
• Trade certification (NABTEB, City & Guilds)
• Apprenticeship documentation & vetting		 Qualified for commercial projects, specialized installations (solar, HVAC), and top recommendation ranking.
GREEN BADGE
Level 3: Corporate		 • Gold Badge requirements +
• Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) certificate
• Tax Identification Number (TIN)		 Authorized for large enterprise tenders, multi-site construction contracts, and government subcontracts.

Pillar B: Milestone Escrow & Dispute Resolution State Machine

Our milestone payment engine eliminates payment anxiety for artisans and abandonment anxiety for clients:

1
Job Milestone Creation

Employer posts project and breaks budget into inspectable milestones (e.g. 30% Foundation, 40% Roofing, 30% Finishing).

2
Upfront Escrow Deposit

Employer deposits milestone funds into TradeLink's neutral escrow vault via bank transfer or card. Funds are locked.

3
Work Execution & Guaranteed Alert

Artisan receives instant automated alert that funds are secured and commences work with guaranteed payout backing.

4
On-Site Inspection & Instant Release

Client inspects completed milestone on-site. Upon approval, funds are disbursed directly to artisan's Nigerian bank account within seconds.

5
Dispute Mediation Vault

If specifications are breached, either party can trigger mediation. Milestone remains frozen while human trade arbiters review evidence and resolve payouts.

Pillar C: Omnichannel Conversational Access (WhatsApp Business Gateway)

Implemented in bot/views.py and bot/handlers.py, TradeLink connects with Meta WhatsApp Cloud API and Twilio. Low-data artisans receive job alerts, submit bids, and communicate with employers without needing high-end smartphones.

5. Security, Data Privacy & NDPA Compliance Architecture

Safeguard Pillar Implementation Specification
Encryption Everywhere Enforced TLS 1.3 across all HTTP endpoints and webhook consumers. Relational data and documents encrypted at rest using AES-256; passwords hashed via salted PBKDF2/Argon2.
Zero Card Storage No debit card numbers, CVVs, or bank account PINs touch TradeLink servers. All payment flows are tokenized via licensed, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateway partners (Flutterwave).
Role-Based Access (RBAC) Strict data segregation. Public clients only see validated badges and aggregate reviews. Private identification (NIN, CAC documents) is strictly restricted to vetted compliance staff.
Testing Isolation Development and staging environments are logically and physically isolated from production. All testing and model validation utilizes anonymized synthetic datasets.
